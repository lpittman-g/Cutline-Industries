2. YouTube Partner Program (YPP) revenue — include all of these

Money source How it pays Cutline Needs
Ad revenue (longform) Skipable/non-skippable in-stream, bumper, overlay, display, and sponsored cards on eligible videos. Shared via Google AdSense. YPP + AdSense linked; content eligible for ads
Shorts ad revenue Revenue share from ads shown in/around Shorts feed for eligible Shorts. YPP Shorts monetization; Shorts volume via Autopilot
YouTube Premium Share of Premium subscriber watch time on your content (no ads shown to them). YPP; audience that uses Premium
Channel memberships Monthly fan subscriptions for badges, emotes, perks, members-only videos/posts. YPP + memberships enabled
Super Chat / Super Stickers Paid highlighted messages during live streams / premieres. YPP + live / premiere
Super Thanks One-time fan support on uploaded videos. YPP + Super Thanks enabled
Shopping / Merch shelf Commission/sales from YouTube Shopping products and merch shelf on watch pages. Shopping eligibility + products connected
YouTube Shopping affiliate Commission when viewers buy tagged products through your videos/lives. Shopping affiliate program access
BrandConnect / official brand deals Paid campaigns matched through YouTube’s brand marketplace (where available). Access + brand-safe channel
YPP thresholds change over time. Blueprint assumes Cutline will qualify (subs + watch hours / Shorts views) then turn on every eligible revenue feature above.

4. System architecture (how content becomes cash)

Production layer

  • Capture VODs / ranked sessions
  • Cutline Studio marks clips
  • Autopilot cuts vertical Shorts (FFmpeg)
  • Titles, hooks, hashtags, CTAs auto-written
  • Publish longform + Shorts + lives on schedule

Distribution layer

  • YouTube channel (primary)
  • Shorts feed for subscriber growth
  • Cross-post clips when useful
  • Website: cutline-industries.studio
  • AWS Amplify hosting + Route 53 DNS

Monetization layer

  • AdSense (ads + Shorts ads + Premium share)
  • Memberships / Super Chat / Super Thanks
  • Shopping + merch shelf
  • BrandConnect + direct sponsors
  • Stripe for products / services

Operations layer

  • Google OAuth + YouTube Data API
  • Secrets / token storage
  • Inbox → cut → upload worker
  • Analytics review (RPM, CTR, retention)
  • Tax / business entity (Atlas / LLC)

Autopilot → Revenue Operating Model

What to run daily so ad revenue and fan money can compound

6. Daily / weekly machine

Daily (Autopilot)

  • Drop VOD in inbox (or overnight recording)
  • Autopilot cuts 5–15 Shorts
  • Upload Shorts (private → public schedule)
  • Pin comment CTA: membership / Super Thanks / product
  • Reply to early comments (retention + community)

Weekly (money ops)

  • 1–2 longform videos for ad inventory
  • 1 live or premiere for Super Chat
  • Members-only perk post
  • Update affiliate + sponsor reads
  • Review RPM, RPM by video, Shorts revenue, top traffic sources

8. Architecture summary (one glance)

Creator / VODs → Cutline Studio + Autopilot (AWS) → YouTube Data API → YouTube Channel → Viewers → YPP Money Stack (Ads · Shorts Ads · Premium · Memberships · Super Chat/Stickers · Super Thanks · Shopping · BrandConnect) → AdSense / Stripe / Invoices → Cutline Industries bank.

Site edge: Squarespace domain cutline-industries.studio → Route 53 → Amplify → Cutline web app / media kit / pay links.

This blueprint is the business + systems map for making money from the channel. Ad rates (CPM/RPM) vary by niche, country, season, and content; volume + retention from Autopilot Shorts and ad-friendly longform are the growth levers.
